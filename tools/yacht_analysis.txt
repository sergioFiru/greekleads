SECTION 9 — KEY INSIGHTS FOR MARKET ANALYSIS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  DATA SOURCE:
  - Greek General Commercial Registry (GEMI) — official government data
  - Covers legally registered businesses only (not informal operators)
  - Primary KAD = main declared business activity

  MARKET STRUCTURE:
  - 4,037 registered charter entities in Greece (GEMI)
  - 3,376 currently active (83.6%)
  - Bareboat (DIY sailing) accounts for 73.0% of all registrations
  - Crewed/skippered charter: 27.0%

  GEOGRAPHY:
  - Most concentrated in ΑΤΤΙΚΗΣ (1,893 firms = 46.9% of market)
  - Top 3 prefectures hold 2,281 firms (56.5% of market)

  DIGITAL OPPORTUNITY:
  - 79% have NO website — massive gap in a tourism-driven business
  - 18% have no registered email
  - 678 firms are completely offline
  - Suggesting most charters rely on word-of-mouth, platforms (GetMyBoat,
    Zizoo, Navtika) or brokers rather than their own digital presence

  CONSOLIDATION:
  - Market is highly fragmented — mostly individual boat owners (ΑΤΟΜΙΚΗ)
    or small IKE/EPE companies with 1-2 vessels
  - Low barrier to entry (register a boat, get a KAD) = many micro-operators
  - Limited corporate players → opportunity for aggregation/platform play
